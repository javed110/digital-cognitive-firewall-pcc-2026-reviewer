## Candidate contribution

An inspectable, local message-review workflow combining visible pressure cues, user-supplied domain context and a verification explanation is a candidate implementation direction. Any benefit of English, Urdu or Roman Urdu support must be tested on an appropriately labelled corpus and with intended users. The combination of cybersecurity, psychology and AI alone does not establish novelty. Comparative accuracy, cost, speed, commercial absence and first-in-country claims remain unestablished.

## Evaluation needed

Freeze a separate held-out corpus with legitimate urgency, benign warnings, quoted attacks, multilingual content and semantic/Unicode evasion. Independent annotators should label intent and persuasion cues, record disagreements and keep duplicate/template families in one split. Compare keyword, technical-cue and combined baselines against a specified trained model. Report class counts, precision, recall, F1, benign-message false-positive rate and uncertainty by language and message family. Fit thresholds only on development data. Record provenance and licensing before using external data.

Any human study needs prospective institutional review, informed consent and a protocol that measures decisions, comprehension, false alarms, response delay and warning burden. A manual panic slider is a simulation input, not a validated psychological measure. Behavioural sensing and message rewriting need separate feasibility, privacy and safety evaluation.
